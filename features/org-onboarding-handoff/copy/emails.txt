            WHAT WE SEND ON YOUR BEHALF
        [h1] The three emails
    Every email we send in your name, in full. Each one has a job and stops — no newsletters, no re-engagement, no unsubscribe-to-escape. These are the only three.
          1 · Admin approval
          to 3 people, once
        Sent the moment somebody registers the org. This is the first thing most admins ever see of OurHike, so it has to survive being read by a sceptical volunteer on a phone.
            Maria named you an admin of Ramapo Trail Conference
            hello@ourhike.org → dana@ramapotrails.org
            Dana — Maria Alvarez has put the Conference's trails on OurHike, a free offline trail map built by volunteers, and named you one of three admins. Nothing is public yet. It needs all three of you to agree.
              WHAT YOU ARE AGREEING TO
              · ramapotrails.org is ours, and we can speak for it
              · three admins: Maria, Joseph, you — any of us can propose changes, all three approve them
              · our own membership and donation pages get linked from our sections
              · no money passes through OurHike, and there is no payout programme to join
              [button] Approve
              [button] Something's wrong
              Declining is a real option.
            It pauses the org rather than deleting anything, and you can say why — "our board hasn't voted" is a perfectly good answer.
            Whoever approves first picks up the next step, so there is a mild advantage to being quick. We will remind you once, in a week, and then never again.
          2 · Registry sign-off
          to the other 2 admins
        Sent when the first admin finishes the registry. It is asking somebody to vouch for 312 sections, so it leads with the summary and the exceptions rather than a wall of rows.
            312 sections are ready for you to check
            hello@ourhike.org → joseph@ramapotrails.org
            Joseph — Maria has built the Conference's hike registry from your ArcGIS server. Before it reaches hikers, all three admins confirm it is accurate. You are the second.
              WHAT CAME OUT OF YOUR OWN FILES
                  Sections read
                  312
                  Clean, no questions
                  294

                  18
                  Featured hikes
                  41
              Read the 18 first.
            Those are the ones where a name was missing or two lines looked like duplicates — Maria made a call on each and her reasoning is attached. The other 294 came straight from your SECT_NAME field.

            Flagging a line sends it back with your note attached — it does not start the registry over. Nothing publishes on two approvals; we need yours and Dana's.
          3 · Volunteer welcome
          to 1,104 people, once
        The one a thousand people read. Designed in full on the Welcome screen — it carries each person's own section and supervisor, and the three things they can do this week.
          Rather than duplicate it here, it lives where you send it from — with the roster counts and the privacy note beside it.

            RULES ALL THREE FOLLOW
            · Plain text works. Every one reads fine with images blocked.
            · One action per email. No digests, no bundling.
            · One reminder at most, then silence.
            · Nobody's name appears in an email to somebody outside the org.
            · Replies reach a person, not a no-reply void.
