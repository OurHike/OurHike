How the data is built

OurHike does not survey trails. It gathers what parks, forests, agencies and trail clubs publish, checks it, and turns it into the files your phone downloads. Closures, warnings and other notices are rebuilt several times a day. Trail lines, waypoints and elevation reach your phone in a release, about once a month.
