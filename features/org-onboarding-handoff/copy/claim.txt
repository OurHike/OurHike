          WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU DO
                1
                  Prove the email
                  We send a code to your address at the org domain. That is the whole check — a working address at carolinamountainclub.org is what makes you one of them.
                2
                  You become the first admin
                  Not the owner — the first of three. You can edit and propose, but nothing publishes until two more admins from your org join you.
                3
                  Name your other two
                  Invite two colleagues and the org is fully yours. Until then the submitted data stays live and unchanged.
            Your trails are already live
            Carolina Mountain Club's 214 sections have been on the map since September, walked by hikers who found them there. Claiming the org does not change what is published — it puts you in charge of what happens to it next.
            If somebody claims an org they should not have
            A claim needs a working address at the org's own domain, and the first claim on an org emails every contact we have for it — including whoever submitted the data. Any of them can dispute it, and a disputed claim is frozen until a human at OurHike looks at it.
