# Photos wanted from the club

The field cards show one photo per problem. Licensed collections (National Park Service,
Forest Service, USGS, Geograph) covered most of them; these are the ones nobody has
published. A phone photo is fine.

**How to shoot:** stand where a volunteer would first notice the problem; get the whole
structure in one frame and the problem in a second, closer one. Daylight, no flash. No
people's faces, no car plates. Send the photo with one line: where, when, and your name as
you'd like it credited. By sending it you agree it can be used in OurHike under CC BY 4.0.

## Nothing found anywhere — top priority
1. **Water source: a spring whose blue blazes or sign are missing or faded** — the side trail with nothing marking it.
2. **Shelter: steps, railing or ramp loose or broken.**
3. **Privy: the shavings bin empty** (or the bag/bucket with nothing in it).

## Only a weak match so far — a real one would be much better
4. Privy: crib full, or a cone that needs knocking down
5. Privy: catholes or toilet paper around a privy that people avoid
6. Puncheon: spikes or nails lifting out of the boards
7. Puncheon: slick boards — leaves or mud on the deck
8. Steps: water pooling on a step
9. Water source: spring box or pipe damaged
10. Water source: a privy or campsite uphill or across the stream from a spring
11. Drainage: a log check dam rotting or missing
12. Parking: a lot where you can't see where the trail starts
13. Kiosk: roof or posts damaged, rotten, leaning

## Covered by an ATC photo, pending ATC's permission
Bear pole / food storage damaged (campsite and shelter), privy base logs rotting, privy
seat broken — the Moose Mountain privy "before" photo and the abandoned bear pole on
appalachiantrail.org. A club photo of any of these makes the permission moot.

## Also welcome
A **sound** moldering privy on the A.T. in NY/NJ, a white blaze at eye height on a
Hudson Valley tree, and a puncheon run in good shape — the "what good looks like" plates
are covered, but a local photo beats a Maine or Maryland one.
