        [h1] Where the work actually gets logged
            Not the desktop screens made narrow. A maintainer standing at a blowdown and a supervisor in a trailhead car park need different things than an admin at a laptop — fewer of them, bigger, and working with no signal.
      RULES THESE FOUR FOLLOW
          One job per screen.
        No tabs, no side panels. If it is not the thing you came to do, it is not here.
          Everything works offline.
        Actions queue and sync later. A screen never blocks on a network it does not have.
          Hit targets 44px and up.
        Assume gloves, low sun and one hand on a trekking pole.
          Never type what we can read.
        Location, weather and section come from where the phone already is.
