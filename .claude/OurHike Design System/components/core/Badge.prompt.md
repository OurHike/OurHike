Small pill label for trail difficulty and status flags on hike listings and cards.

```jsx
<Badge tone="easy">Easy</Badge>
<Badge tone="moderate">Moderate</Badge>
<Badge tone="strenuous">Strenuous</Badge>
```

Tones map to trail-difficulty convention (easy/moderate/strenuous) plus `info` and `neutral` for general status tags (e.g. "Closed", "New").
