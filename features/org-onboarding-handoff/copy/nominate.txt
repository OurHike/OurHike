          HOW THIS GOES
              1
              You give us their website. Claude finds the data.
              2
              You sign in to GitHub and open the pull request under your own name.
              3
              We email three people at the club. Three have to approve.
              4
              If they accept, the club owns it from then on — not you.
            You are a proposer, not an admin
            Opening this gives you no standing at the club and no control over their trails. Your name stays on the pull request as the person who offered — that is the whole of your role, and it is a generous one.
