        Your trails stay on your website. We just draw them.
      This is the part clubs miss until they see it.
        You do not send hikers to OurHike
      — you paste one line of HTML into your own pages and these four things appear there, in your fonts and colours, under your own domain. Everything below is live, running the Throughikers' data, and it is the same code an org pastes in.
          It stops going stale
          A hand-written hikes page is out of date the week you publish it. These read your signed-off registry, so they change when your trails do and never otherwise.
          Your traffic stays yours
          The membership and donation links inside them are yours, not ours. No cookies, no tracking, and no OurHike branding you cannot remove.
          No project to run
          One line of HTML and a script tag — no CMS plugin, no rebuild, no developer on retainer. If our servers are down your page still renders; the embed just does not appear.
