Field guide plates
One plate per issue, as the app shows them one at a time. Plates traced from photographs where a photograph exists (sign, blaze); the rest are stand-in sketches until the club's photos arrive. See the shot list in the Field Guide doc.
Bridge
Don't use it. The Local Chair is told the moment it uploads.
What good looks like
Abutments eroded or scoured
Loose or missing bolts
Cracked beams or logs
Broken or spongy decking
Damaged railings
Rot
Puncheon
Same as a bridge: don't use it, and the Local Chair is told when it uploads.
What good looks like
Sleeper sunk, section tilted
Spongy or broken boards
Loose fasteners
Rot
Slick, leaves or mud on the deck
Shelter
Don't sleep in it. A Poor or Unsafe shelter also raises a Shelter repair report for the crew.
What good looks like
Roof leaking, sagging or missing shingles
Floor, walls or deck rotten or broken
Steps, railings or ramp loose
Fire ring: more than one, oversized, full of ash
Food storage (box, cables, pole) missing or broken
A hazard tree over the site
Privy
A floor or seat that can't be used. Everything else is Poor.
What good looks like
Out of shavings
Crib full, or a cone to knock down
Too wet, or smells wrong
Floor or base logs rotting
Seat broken
Door, roof, vent damaged
Catholes around it
Campsite
A hazard tree over a tent site. Otherwise a campsite is Poor at worst.
What good looks like
Extra fire rings that visitors built
Ring oversized, or built up with ash
Site spreading: new paths, trampled plants
Platform or pad damaged
Food storage damaged or missing
Hazard trees over the site
Water source
Not for a bad smell or colour: nobody is in danger from the structure. Write what you suspect in the note.
What good looks like
Flow: flowing, trickle, or dry
Blue blazes or the sign missing
Pipe or box damaged
Something could foul it: a privy or camp across the stream
Access path eroded
Blaze
A blaze is never Unsafe. If you can't see the next one, that's Poor, and the maintainer should hear about it.
What good looks like
Faded, flaking, damaged or the wrong shape
Can't see the next one from here
The tree came down
Leaves or brush across the sightline
An old blaze never sprayed out
A visitor's rock stack
Sign
A sign is never Unsafe. Log a damaged or missing one as Poor.
What good looks like
Missing
Damaged, faded or unreadable
Post rotten, loose or leaning
Out of date, or a temporary sign left up
Flyers, ads or stickers on it
Kiosk
A kiosk that could fall on someone. Everything else is Poor.
What good looks like
Content out of date
Panel faded, torn or stained
Cover cracked, missing or unlocked
Roof or posts damaged, rotten, leaning
QR stickers, ads, flyers
A sign that should be on it and isn't
Parking
Only if the lot itself is dangerous: an entrance washed out, a tree down across it.
What good looks like
Litter or dumping
Trailhead hard to find from the lot
Sign or kiosk damaged
Access blocked
Cars on an unsafe pull-off outside the lot
Steps
A rocking step on a steep pitch. The Local Chair is told when it uploads.
What good looks like
Stone loose, missing, settled or eroded
Timber rotten
Water pooling on a step
Hikers walking around it
Buried in leaves or gravel
Something else
Rarely: a collapsed wall or crossing. A clogged water bar is Poor.
What good looks like
Water bar clogged on the uphill side, or its outlet blocked
Water bar walked around, or with no downhill exit
Checkdam wood rotting or missing
Drainage dip filled in
Cairn knocked down, or a visitor's rock stack where none belongs
