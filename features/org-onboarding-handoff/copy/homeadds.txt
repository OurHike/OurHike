      WHY THIS WORDING
      ·
        “Do you maintain these trails?”
      — a question, because the reader is a hiker until the moment they realise it is about them. A headline like “For organisations” filters itself out before it is read.
      ·
        “instead of from us guessing”
      — names the thing that actually annoys a trails chair about every other hiking app.
      ·
        No mention of GIS, registries or pull requests.
      Those are true and they belong on the next page, not on a homepage card.
