Six Hundred Fountains, One Screen

New York City's drinking fountains are on the map and the map cannot hold them. This is the worst screen the city produces, measured rather than imagined, and four ways to fix it — three of which turn out to fix a different half of the problem than the one you notice first.

Release 2026-09-16-4 nearby_poi.geojson · 20,444 features 3,148 fountains · 758 restrooms #1534 · decided #1536 · building 1 + 2

§0what shipped

The maintainer took options 1 and 2 on 2026-09-17, and two things changed on the way in

This page is the record of the options, not of the build. Its frames and figures are left exactly as they were when the decision was made; #1536 — Give the pins air and fold New York City's co-located fountains, which is the maintainer's pick of options 1 and 2 is what was actually built, and features/POI_VISIBILITY.md's open question carries the reasoning. Two departures are worth reading here, beside the pictures that prompted them.

Option 1's padding is 36, not the 24 drawn below, and it ramps. This page applies one flat padding to every mark on the screen. Measured afterwards, a blanket padding costs the Appalachian Trail half its pins at z9 — 20 down to 10 — so the shipped rule is per-feature, off until a waypoint has more neighbours than anything on the A.T. has, and a ramp that leaves the trail alone has to spend more at its top to reach the same place. End to end the real figures are 374 marks, 32 pins, 15% of the screen under pin, against this page's 375 / 23 / 11%.

The pins below wear a count and the shipped ones do not. Every New York City fountain ships at confidence_floor: low, so a mark decorated to say 12 offers reassurance the source cannot supply. The pin says water is here; the count and what is unknown about it belong on the card. reasoned

One thing the drawing got wrong and the build does not: the folded marks here sit at their group's centroid. A centroid of twelve fountains is a point on a lawn with no fountain on it, and a hiker walks to the pin — so the shipped fold anchors on the most central fountain that actually exists.

§1the screen

The densest phone screen in the five boroughs

Brooklyn, centred on -73.9715, 40.6932 — Red Hook to Prospect Park, 3.51 × 6.30 miles of ground at z12. 638 waypoints fall inside it: 531 drinking fountains and 107 public restrooms, and nothing else, because shelters and campsites do not exist here.

measured Counted 2026-09-17 against the published artifact a hiker downloads today, filtered to the four categories a fresh install opens with (DEFAULT_SHOWN_TYPES: shelter, water, campsite, privy). The window is found by spike_city_poi_density.py's point-anchored sweep, which is exact rather than grid-phased. Placement is MapLibre's rule as poiLayers.ts configures it: a 38 px pin at the z12 point of the icon-size ramp, icon-padding: 2, symbols considered in POI_PRIORITY order, a box skipped when it overlaps one already placed.

The comparable figure already in the tree is 656 — features/NEARBY_TRAILS.md §10, measured against the release #1474 — Draw the seam around what a cell carries, ship the merge provenance that did not, and give New York City its restrooms and its fountains published, at a window 1.5 km west of this one. 638 is the same measurement against a later release; the two do not disagree.

§2the diagnosis

"Cramped" is two problems wearing one word

The pins. 63 of them are placed, and they cover 30% of the screen. Their nearest-neighbour distance is a median 51 px when a pin at this zoom is 36 px wide — which is to say they are very nearly touching, edge to edge, with 15 px of paper between them. features/POI_VISIBILITY.md's own arithmetic says about 16 pins fit down the column at a hiking zoom. measured

The dots. 575 more waypoints lost their collision and drew as dots instead, which is the design working — a pin or a dot and never as neither. At 575 on one screen the dot rank stops reading as a stipple and starts reading as a blue wash. measured

The finding that reorders the options

The obvious fix is to stop drawing fountain pins at planning zooms and let them arrive later. Measured, it barely helps: 63 pins becomes 50. The restrooms simply take the space the fountains vacated.

icon-allow-overlap: false packs pins until nothing else fits. It has no notion of enough — so the screen is full because the placement rule fills it, not because water is dense. Any fix aimed at the water layer alone moves the author of the crowding and not the crowding. measured

That splits the option space cleanly, and the split is the useful part of this page:

  • Folding waypoints together barely moves the pin count. All three folding options below land between 53 and 61 pins, against today's 63. What they move is the dots — 575 down to 314, 217, 58.
  • Only spacing moves the pins. 63 down to 22, and nothing else here does that.

Which means the two levers are not alternatives. They fix different halves. (53, 58 and 61 pins, folded three ways.)

§3four options

Four options, drawn on the same screen

OPTION 1

Give the pins air

Raise icon-padding from 2 to 24, so a pin claims an 82 px box rather than a 40 px one and the collision engine stops at 22 placements instead of 63. Nothing merges and nothing is claimed. Every fountain keeps its own dot at its own coordinate and its own tap target; 41 of them trade a pin for a dot, and the dot was already there under the pin.

What it costs

A hiker scanning for the blue droplet has fewer droplets to scan. The information is not gone — it is a dot, at the true place, opening the same card — but the pin is the shape that reads at arm's length and 41 fewer of them read.

unvalidated 24 px is picked, not measured. It is the value that lands this screen near POI_VISIBILITY.md's ~16, and no more than that. What would settle it is the same thing POI_DOT_RADIUS_EXPRESSION is still waiting on: a look at a real phone in real sunlight, which nobody has done (#105 — Check the map's marks on a real phone in real sunlight).

It is also the cheapest change on this page — one expression in buildPoiLayer, and it can be scoped by zoom so the corridor is untouched.

OPTION 2

Fold co-located fountains into sites

The mechanism pipeline/sources.json already names for this problem, and it is built and shipping: features/POI_SITES.md's two gates, which fold a privy onto its shelter's pin along the A.T. Ported here, the gates become same park property AND within 80 m — the published fountain already carries its park name, 912 distinct names over 3,148 fountains, so the naming evidence ATC supplies is supplied by NYC Parks.

638 marks become 375; the largest fold on this screen is 12 fountains. Each site has a stable id computed in the pipeline, so it survives a pan, a zoom and a reload, and a report or a field note can point at it. measured

What it costs

export_poi.py says it already: a wrong grouping is baked into the artifact and a hiker cannot undo it. That is why the rule needs both gates rather than proximity alone.

unvalidated 80 m is picked. POI_SITES.md's own proximity gate is 60 m, chosen against ATC's shelter geometry rather than a playground's. What would settle it is the distribution of fountain-to-fountain distance inside one park property — a one-query measurement this page did not run.

On its own it leaves the pin count at 61. It is the half that fixes the dots.

OPTION 3

Fold to the park, unfold on the way in

One mark per park property below z13 — Prospect Park · 55 — then sites, then individual fountains. It collapses harder than option 2: 638 marks become 275.

What it costs, and it is the reason this is third

measured At z14 — a zoom a hiker walks at — Central Park's 85 fountains fold into one mark at a centroid. A centroid is not a place you can walk to. On the water path that is precisely the confidently wrong answer FEATURES.md calls more dangerous than an honest unknown, and the unfold thresholds are the only thing standing between the design and it.

OPTION 4

Cluster in screen space

The generic map-library answer: group anything within 44 px of a cluster head, recompute on every camera change. It collapses hardest of all — 638 marks become 111, and it is the only option that visibly clears the dot wash.

What it costs

This repository has already written the argument against it, in pipeline/lib/poi_sites.py's header: a group computed on a phone "has no id that survives a pan, re-clusters at every zoom, and answers 'how many' when the question at a shelter is 'is there a privy'." Every word of that holds for a fountain. It is on this page because it is the option somebody will propose, and it deserves its measurement rather than a dismissal.

§4recommendation

Take 1 and 2, in that order

Air first, sites second — they are not alternatives

Option 1 is the only lever that moves the pins and is a one-expression change. Option 2 is the only lever that makes the remaining dots mean something, and it is a mechanism that already exists and already ships. Together on this screen: 375 marks, 23 pins, 11% of the screen under pin, largest fold 12.

Shipping them separately is fine and the order matters: option 1 alone is a visible improvement on the same day it lands, and option 2 is the pipeline work that wants a measurement first (the 80 m above).

§6not answered

What none of these options touches

A folded site of twelve unknowns is still twelve unknowns

Every New York City fountain ships at confidence_floor: low, and the reason is in sources.json: featuresta reads Active on all 3,849 rows, zero variance, so the layer carries nothing about whether any given fountain works.

Folding interacts with that and this page does not resolve it. One mark standing for 12 fountains nobody has confirmed reads more confident than 12 marks nobody has confirmed — a count is a fact about the inventory and a hiker will read it as a fact about the water. Whichever option is taken, the fold needs a decision about what a low-confidence count says on the card, and it should be made deliberately rather than inherited from the A.T. shelter case where confidence is high. reasoned

  • This is one screen. The worst one the city produces at z12, found exactly — but the option that is best here is not automatically best in Flushing Meadows or on the Coney Island boardwalk, where the fountains run in lines rather than in banks.
  • The corridor is untouched by all of it. Every number here is inside the five boroughs. Nothing proposed above should change what a hiker sees on the A.T., and options 1 and 3 both need a scope — by zoom, by source, or by measured local density — that keeps it that way. That scope is unspecified here and is real design work. reasoned
