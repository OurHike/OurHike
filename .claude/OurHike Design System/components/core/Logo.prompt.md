OurHike's real brand mark ("4a — Dual-Tone Horizontal Lockup"): a rounded-square icon split diagonally into forest green and stone brown, with a white torn-edge trail-blaze capsule on top, paired with the "OurHike" wordmark (Public Sans 800). High-fidelity — colors and proportions are final, not a placeholder.

```jsx
<Logo />
<Logo size={44} iconOnly />
```

`size` scales the icon (and, unless `iconOnly`, the wordmark and gap) proportionally from the 96px reference — use for the full lockup (headers, onboarding) or `iconOnly` (favicons, app icons, compact nav).
