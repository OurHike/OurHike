        Unpublish a trail
        Take a single trail off the map without leaving. Hikers stop seeing it within the hour; the history stays in the repo, because a merged commit cannot be unsaid.
        [button] Choose a trail to unpublish
        Delete this org
        This removes the org and everybody's admin access.
          It does not delete the trails.
        They revert to unclaimed — still on the map for hikers, with nobody behind them — and anybody with an email at your domain can adopt them later from Claim your org.
          WHAT SURVIVES YOU
          ·
            Your trails stay published
          — removing them is a separate decision, above.
          ·
            Volunteers keep their own records.
          Their hours and reports are theirs, not yours to delete.
          ·
            The repo history remains.
          Every past change stays readable, which is what made it trustworthy in the first place.
