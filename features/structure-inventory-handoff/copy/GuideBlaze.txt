What good looks like
White, 2 by 6 in, sharp corners, eye height, usually on the right. From each one you can see the next.
Photo: Shenandoah NPS · public domain
Unsafe means
A blaze is never Unsafe. If you can't see the next one, that's Poor, and the maintainer should hear about it.
Repainting is the maintainer's job, above 50°F (NYNJTC). You log it.
ATC Marking the Trail · NYNJTC Trail Maintenance Manual 8.5 · NYNJTC Guidelines for Blazing Trails
