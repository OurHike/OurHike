            This is not a credential
            ATC's Ridgerunners are staff. You are a volunteer trail monitor, and the difference matters on the ground: a hiker must never take a closure, a fire rule or a camping instruction from you as though it came from the agency. So nothing is issued that works as a badge — no card, no public profile, no pin showing where you are.
